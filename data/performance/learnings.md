## Rules (strongest evidence first)
1. **Never open with a date or a place, and never let a date be the first words after the hook.** In 5 Reels the average viewer leaves on a dated line: ember-full at 9 s ("1638. A storm over Dartmoor"), ember-cut-real opens on that same line (70.6% skip), lodestone-cut-real opens on "1269. A French army besieges Lucera" (70.9%), fact-02 left at 3.4 s just as card 1 "1959, Greenland: …" appeared, and LODESTONE left on its 2nd dated beat. Both cuts that open on a date skipped >70%, the worst cuts on the channel. Put the date in a stat chip or after the strange thing. Exception: ember-cut-number opens on "In 2007, a team in Brazil" at 48.1% skip, but frame 0 was a full data chart (rule 2).
2. **Frame 0 must be full: the device, the evidence or the claim, big and readable. Never a near-empty page.** Best openers: LODESTONE (moving magnet wheel + claim) 42.1%, HULL 7 43.7%, ember-cut-number (spectrometer chart with Si/Fe/Ca lines, "900 m", then "2007 · LAB · BRAZIL") 48.1%, the best cut so far. Worst board openers are a small sketched ball on a blank page: ember-full 61.5%, ember-cut-stamp 69.8%. 5 Reels.
3. **Films: the question by 10 s, one dated beat.** Film watch ratios run 0.086–0.203. EMBER (question at 65.6 s) got 0.086, the worst film so far, and its reach (125) was the lowest film reach. LODESTONE and HULL 7 put the device and stakes up front and got 0.20. v4 encodes this; lifter is the first test (not yet posted).
4. **Show the result first, explain after.** Every cut lost its average viewer during setup, before the payoff. ember-cut-number left at 7 s ("touches an electric arc…"), before the three "Held." stamps at ~16 s. ember-cut-stamp left at 4 s, and the CLEARED stamp came at ~19 s. Also cut-why, cut-trials, cut-catch and the teaser (earlier weeks). 6 Reels.
5. **"Number" is the best cut hook type so far.** It went 2 for 2 above the cut median: ember-cut-number 48.1% / 7.2 s / reach 187 and lodestone-cut-trials 58.1% / 8.1 s / reach 171. These are the 2 highest cut reaches and the 2 longest cut holds. "this_was_real" as built (opening on the date) went 0 for 2 (70.6%, 70.9%). A stamp cut that doesn't show the stamp up front failed (ember 69.8%), while the one that did (lodestone-cut-catch 55.6%) was fine.
6. **The v4 moving opener did not fix the fact shorts.** fact-02 (rays, specks, icon push-in) got 70.2% skip and 3.4 s, against fact-01 (still) at 64.3% and 3.9 s. Fact shorts are the worst kind on the channel (median 67.3% skip, 3.6 s). All cards-kit shorts are unvoiced (music only). The 5 unvoiced shorts have a median of 63.6% skip and 4.2 s, against 58.1% and 5.9 s for the narrated cuts. That points one way but is not proven, because game-01 was unvoiced and held 8.5 s.
7. **Don't reuse the same opening seconds within a week.** ember-cut-question used the same first 7 s as the film (4 days earlier). Instagram showed it to **9** accounts, against a short baseline of 124–187. It is n=1 and the cause is not proven, but it was the only change. ember-cut-stamp reused the same opening and got 69.8% skip.
8. **A comment prompt must be on screen before ~6 s, with the answer held back.** Only game-pigeon (answer held to the next day) has earned a comment (1). The caption questions on the films, cuts and fact-02 ("Would you have lived down there?") got 0 comments across 6 Reels this week.
