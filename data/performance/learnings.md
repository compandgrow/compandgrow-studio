## Rules (strongest evidence first)
1. **Never open with a date, and never let a date be the first words of the first beat.** In 7 Reels the average viewer leaves on a dated line: ember-full ("1638…"), ember-cut-real (70.6% skip), lodestone-cut-real (70.9%), fact-02 (left at 3.4 s on "1959, Greenland"), LODESTONE's 2nd dated beat, and now **fact-03** (left at 6.3 s on "1970, Soviet Union: drilling starts…", beat 1 3.0–6.6 s) and **fact-05** (left at 6.0 s on "1942: a dentist mails the idea…", 3.0–6.6 s). Put dates in the stat chip. Beat 1 = the biggest number or the payoff.
2. **Show the "no way" thing first, explain after.** STILLWING opens "This foil triangle flies. Nothing on it moves." and got 36.2% skip, the best on the channel. fact-03/05 hooks name a strange physical act ("drilled 12 km into the Earth", "turn bats into bombs") and got 41.8% / 44.4% skip, against 64–70% for fact-01/02. Every cut and fact short still loses its average viewer before the payoff (fact-05's "bats set their own base on fire" sits at 13.8 s). 9 Reels.
3. **Frame 0 must be full: the device, the evidence or the claim, big and readable.** Best openers: STILLWING 36.2%, LODESTONE 42.1%, HULL 7 43.7%, ember-cut-number 48.1%. Worst: a small sketch on a blank page (ember-full 61.5%, ember-cut-stamp 69.8%). 7 Reels.
4. **Brain tests: ask for a number, and show the ask early.** brain-02 ("How many times does gear C turn? COMMENT A NUMBER") got 2 comments, 1 share and 5 saves on 535 reach, the best value and most comments of any short. brain-01 (A/B clockwise, a 50/50 guess) got 0 of each on 234 reach, though it held best (0.39). On both, the average viewer left during the timer (5.8 s / 7.4 s), before the "COMMENT YOUR ANSWER" lock screen at 13–15 s. Put the ask on the hook card and shorten the timer to ~8 s. 2 Reels (trial continues).
5. **Films: device first, the question by 10 s, physics explained fast.** STILLWING (v4: 99 s, the question "So is it antigravity?" at 7.4 s) is at 21.8 s held / 0.22 ratio / 18.9 value per 1k after 15 h. That is the best value of any Reel and the 2nd-best hold after LODESTONE (24.2 s). EMBER (question at 65.6 s) got 0.086. The average STILLWING viewer left on "The sharp wire's field ionises the air." (20.4–23.6 s), the first technical line. 4 films + 1 early.
6. **"Number" is the best cut hook type.** It went 2 for 2: ember-cut-number 48.1% skip and lodestone-cut-trials 58.1%, with the 2 highest cut reaches. this_was_real opening on a date went 0 for 2 (70.8%). lifter-cut-real (Nov 25) opens on device + claim, not a date. It is the test of whether the label or the date was the problem.
7. **Don't reuse the same opening seconds within a week.** ember-cut-question reused the film's first 7 s and Instagram showed it to 9 accounts. n=1, but it was the only change.
8. **The comment prompt has to be on screen, and the answer has to be worth typing.** Comments so far: brain-02 2 (number answer), game-pigeon 1 (held-back answer), lodestone-full 1. Caption-only questions ("Would you have gone deeper?", "Real or too weird to be true?") got 0 on all 8 Reels that used them.
(Standing note: Instagram/Zernio returns 0 follows, 0 profile views and 0 replays on every Reel, even in a +8 week. Judge follows by account-level timing until per-post follows appear.)
