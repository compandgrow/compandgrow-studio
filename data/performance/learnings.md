## Rules (strongest evidence first)
1. **Viewers leave during the history beat, 13–22 s in. Get the device's question on screen before it.** Evidence: avg watch lands inside the dated-history lines on all 3 films: HULL 7 21.4 s ("By 1940, hulls were wrapped in cable"), LONGSHOT 13.3 s ("On his 78th birthday"), LODESTONE (early) 20.3 s ("In it: a wheel that magnets would drive around forever"). All three film watch ratios are under 0.2. The question/stakes line comes at 30 s (HULL 7) or later. Scriptwriter: question or payoff tease in the first 10 s, then one dated beat at most before the physics.
2. **A first line needs a subject: a problem or a device, not a bare number.** Evidence: "Every ship ever built / has the same weakness" (HULL 7) 43.7% skip, "A wheel of magnets. No fuel. No plug." (LODESTONE film/teaser) 43–57% skip, "250 miles." (LONGSHOT, 2 words in 3 s) 68.4% skip. 3 Reels.
3. **In multi-segment cuts, the next segment opens on a question or a number, never on an explanation.** Evidence: the teaser held to 7.26 s of 18, right at the splice (7.3 s) into "Friction takes the rest..." 1 Reel, so treat it as a working rule, not a proven one.
4. **Nothing yet gives a reason to follow.** 0 follows, 0 comments, 1 save across 5 Reels. Every outro/caption question ("Would you sign off on it?") got 0 comments, and so did the game's "Comment 1, 2 or 3". Not a rule yet, a known gap: test an explicit series hook ("Case 5 next Sunday") in the outro.
