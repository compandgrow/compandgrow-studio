## Rules (strongest evidence first)
1. **Films lose the average viewer in the history block. One dated beat, then the question, by 10 s.** Average watch lands inside the dated-history lines on all 4 films: HULL 7 21.4 s ("By 1940…"), LONGSHOT 13.3 s ("On his 78th birthday"), LODESTONE 24.2 s ("In the 1600s, English scholar John Wilkins…", the 2nd of 3 dated beats; the question came at 50.5 s), EMBER (early) 10.4 s ("1638. A storm over Dartmoor"). Film watch ratios run 0.10–0.20. v4 already encodes this; the lifter film (Nov 15) is the first test.
2. **Frame 0 shows the strange thing (device/claim), never a date + place, a label, a definition or a bare number.** Date + place opener "1269. A French army besieges Lucera" → 70.9% skip (lodestone-cut-real, a clear failure). Label "Trial one. Bare magnets" → 58.1%. Bare number "250 miles." → 68.4%. Device + claim "A wheel of magnets. No fuel. No plug." → 42.1% (best on the channel). 4 Reels.
3. **Motion at frame 0 holds; still cards don't.** The still-card shorts (fact-01, game-pigeon, reveal-pigeon) skip 63–64%, and held 3.9–5.9 s. lodestone-cut-trials (spinning wheel + ticking counter from frame 0) held 8.1 s, the longest of any cut, with the most cut reach (171). Supports v4's "moving opener" rule; fact-02 tonight is the first test.
4. **Show the result first, explain after.** Every short lost its average viewer during the setup, before the payoff: cut-why left at the "+5/−5" bookkeeping (6 s) before the wheel STOPS (15 s); cut-trials left in trial 2 (8 s) before "40 days. Still turning." (~20 s); cut-catch left at 4.6 s, before "It has a plug." (~14 s); teaser left at the friction explanation. 4 Reels. Number cuts open on the end state, explainer cuts on the outcome.
5. **Every Reel must make sense to a stranger.** With 9 followers, nearly every viewer is new. "Yesterday, one of these was real." (reveal) 63.3% skip, with 0 sign anyone came back from the game (reach 131 ≈ baseline, 0 follows/comments). "One catch." with no claim: best cut hook (55.6%), but held only 0.24 because the battery line meant nothing cold. "So why…" opens on a continuation word. 3 Reels.
6. **A comment prompt must be on screen before ~6 s, and the answer must be held back.** game-01: vote at ~15 s, answer in the same Reel → 0 comments. game-pigeon: answer held to the next day → 1 comment (7.2/1k), the first a game has earned. Both lost the average viewer before the vote (8.5 s, 5.9 s). Applies to the brain test: puzzle + "comment your answer" + timer visible from frame 0.
7. **Shorts' reach is flat at 126–171 whatever the content; only films break 300.** 9 shorts: 126–171 reach. Films: 395 (HULL 7), 340 (LODESTONE), 156 (LONGSHOT, 68% skip). Day 1 is ~all of the lifetime reach on every Reel (e.g. LODESTONE 333 of 340, cut-real 132 of 132). Daily posting added Reels but not reach per Reel or followers.
8. **Nothing yet gives a reason to follow.** 0 follows from ~2,340 reached across 12 Reels, even with follow cards on every v3 short ("New one every week", "Don't miss the reveal"). Not solved by an end card the average viewer never reaches (the average short viewer leaves at 4–8 s).
