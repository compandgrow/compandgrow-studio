## True core leads (researcher verifies each; claim -> source URL in FACTS.md)
- **NIST 2010, aluminium-ion optical clocks:** raising one clock 33 cm made it run measurably faster; the release says this adds up to about 90 billionths of a second over a 79-year lifetime ("your head is older than your feet"). Also measured motion time dilation at a few metres per second. Science, 24 Sep 2010 (Chou et al.). Sources: https://www.nist.gov/news-events/news/2010/09/nist-pair-aluminum-atomic-clocks-reveal-einsteins-relativity-personal ; https://ui.adsabs.harvard.edu/abs/2010Sci...329.1630C. **Best candidate for the one dated beat.**
- **Hafele–Keating, Oct 1971:** cesium clocks flown around the world on commercial airliners, eastward and westward, compared with clocks at the US Naval Observatory; eastward clocks lost time (~59 ns), westward gained (~273 ns), roughly as predicted. Sources: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hafele%E2%80%93Keating_experiment ; https://hyperphysics.gsu.edu/hbase/Relativ/airtim.html ; original Science 1972 papers. Good backup / this_was_real cut material.
- **GPS clock correction:** satellite clocks run ~38 microseconds/day fast relative to the ground and are corrected for it, or positions would drift by kilometres per day. Lead: standard figure (Ashby, "Relativity in the Global Positioning System", Living Reviews in Relativity 2003). Verify the 38 µs and the "km per day" figure before using it.
- **JILA 2022:** time dilation measured across about 1 mm within a single atom cloud (Bothwell et al., Nature, Feb 2022). Lead only; verify. Great "number" line if confirmed.
- (Search queries for the researcher: "NIST 2010 optical clock 33 cm Science Chou", "Hafele Keating 1971 results nanoseconds", "GPS relativity 38 microseconds per day Ashby", "JILA 2022 time dilation millimeter Nature Bothwell".)

## Fictional program sketch (invented; never attributed to NIST, JILA, USNO, NASA or any real person)
**LONGHOUR**: a sealed chamber whose fictional "mass ring" (a dense rotating ring around the cabin) makes time run slower inside.
Exploded spec sheet: 01 reference clock (outside) · 02 cabin clock (inside) · 03 mass ring · 04 pressure shell · 05 seat. Spec line: "Cabin clock: -1 s per day".
Trials (escalating, inside clock vs outside clock):
- TRIAL 01: 24 h run. Inside clock behind by 1 second. CONFIRMED.
- TRIAL 02: 24 h run, ring at full speed. Behind by 1 hour. CONFIRMED.
- TRIAL 03: crewed run. Volunteer spends 1 day inside; 1 week passes outside. CONFIRMED.
- Honest catch: "One catch. Inside, nothing feels slow." The volunteer gets no extra time: her watch, her heartbeat, her thoughts all slow together. She just steps out into the future, and she can't come back. (True physics shape: time dilation only ever moves you forward relative to others.)
- Stamp: **CLEARED: ONE-WAY TRIP TO THE FUTURE.** (Same twist shape as LODESTONE / STILLWING.)
