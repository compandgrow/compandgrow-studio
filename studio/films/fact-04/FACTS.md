## Dropped
- Crossing Autobahn 61, the river Erft and a railway during the 2001 move — only Wikipedia (uncited sentence); German Wikipedia and a second page could not be opened. Do not use.
- Height (museum 84 m vs Wikipedia/Gizmodo 96 m / 315 ft) and length (220 m vs 225 m) — disagree.
- "Heaviest land vehicle in the world" — Wikipedia/thyssenkrupp says it held the title from 1978 and was overtaken by Bagger 293 in 1995. Record claim with one source and a qualifier → not used.
- 240,000 tons of coal a day (Gizmodo/Wikipedia) vs 265,000 tons of lignite (PopSci) — use the volume figure only.
- Crew of five (Wikipedia citing a Flickr photo), 16.56 MW power, travel speed 2–10 m/min (uncited) — weak sources.
