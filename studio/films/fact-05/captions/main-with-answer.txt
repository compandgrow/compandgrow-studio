The US tried to turn bats into bombs.

January 1942: a dentist mails the idea to the White House. The plan was approved, and in August 1943 the Navy took it over and named it Project X-Ray. It cost about $2 million before it was cancelled.
Earlier, in May 1943, some armed bats escaped and set their own test base on fire.
