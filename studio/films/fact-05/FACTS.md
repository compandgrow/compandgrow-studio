# FACT 05 — "The US military tried to make bat bombs" (Project X-Ray) — facts

All claims are real. No fiction in this piece. Pages opened 2026-11-09 (WebFetch); the quoted line is the text on the page.

Tone guard (never-list / weapons-of-mass-harm): keep it an absurd-engineering story. No payload chemistry (no "napalm"), no target cities, no casualty talk, no real person framed as a villain. Name no individual on screen ("a dentist"), and leave the President and Admiral out of the cards.

## Dropped
- "…and a general's car" — only Air & Space Forces; Wikipedia's source says the test range / fuel tank. Use "set their own test base on fire" (both agree). The car can only appear as "described in Air & Space Forces Magazine", better not at all.
- Bat weight / payload — Air & Space Forces "about nine grams" vs Wikipedia 14 g bat with 15–18 g payload. Disagree → dropped.
- Cancellation year and the reason (atomic bomb) — single uncited line; also too close to weapons-of-mass-harm. Say only "cancelled".
- "Village simulating Japanese structures burned" — target-test detail, off-tone; dropped.
- Number of bats per casing / per drop — not stated consistently.
