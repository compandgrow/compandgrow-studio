# GAME 02 — "Real or fake?" — facts and fiction

Two of the three devices are FAKE and are labelled FAKE on screen at the reveal, with the briefing they come from:
- #1 "A foil wing that flew 12 km. Silent." Fictional, from our STILLWING briefing (see studio/films/lifter/FACTS.md). STILLWING and its 12 km trial are invented. The reveal must say the real 2018 MIT ion plane flew about 60 m, the length of a gym (sourced in lifter/FACTS.md), and the 12 km wing is ours.
- #2 "A power cell running on caught ball lightning." Fictional, from our EMBER briefing (see studio/films/ember/FACTS.md). EMBER and its 6 h cell are invented.

## Dropped (sources disagree or not needed)
- Exact height ceiling (3 ft vs about 2 m): not shown as a number.
- Top speed (35 mph appears on NMUSAF and Flying, but the Edwards article links it to the Dec 1961 finding). Not needed. Leave it out to keep the card to 8 words.
- Cancellation month (September vs December 1961): year only.
- Contract sums ($2 M, $700,000, $1.77 M; Wikipedia only), the predicted "nearly 10,000 feet" (curator quote, Edwards), and supersonic ambitions: not shown.
- Any UFO / "secret saucer" / cover-up framing: never-list guardrail. The card says "built for the US military", nothing more.
