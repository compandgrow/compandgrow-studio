# FACT 06 — "This lab experiment started in 1927. Still running." (Pitch Drop Experiment) — facts

All claims are real. No fiction in this piece. Pages opened 2026-11-09 (WebFetch); the quoted line is the text on the page. Primary source: The University of Queensland, School of Mathematics and Physics (the experiment's owner).

Wording guard: "nobody has seen one fall" must mean **this** experiment (UQ). Other pitch-drop experiments exist elsewhere (a Trinity College Dublin one is reported to have filmed a drop in 2013; not sourced this run, kept only as a reason for caution), so never say "nobody has ever seen pitch drip". Card used (reviewer fix 2026-11-09): "AND NOBODY HAS SEEN ONE FALL HERE." under the UQ funnel.
"Still running": Guinness "has been running since 1927"; UQ "To date, only 9 drops have fallen". If the scriptwriter wants "almost 100 years": 1927 → 2026 = 99 years; fine.

Source caption: "Sources: University of Queensland · Guinness World Records"
