This lab experiment started in 1927. Still running.

The Pitch Drop Experiment at the University of Queensland: pitch looks solid but is a liquid, 100 billion times more viscous than water. The funnel was cut open in 1930, and the first drop took 8 years. Only 9 drops have fallen since.
Nobody has seen one fall there: in 2000, a glitch lost the footage. Guinness calls it the world's longest-running lab experiment.
