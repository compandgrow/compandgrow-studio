# GAME 01 — "Real or fake?" — facts and fiction

Two of the three devices are FAKE and are labelled FAKE on screen at the reveal, with the briefing they come from:
- #1 "A warship hull that bends light around itself. Not seen at 8 km." — fictional, from our HULL 7 briefing (see studio/films/cloak-hull/FACTS.md).
- #3 "8 tuned drivers that bring down a 60 m tower." — fictional, from our TREMOR briefing (see studio/films/tremor/FACTS.md).

The REAL one (#2):

| Claim on screen | Source |
|---|---|
| Project Habakkuk: a British WWII plan for an aircraft carrier made of ice mixed with wood pulp ("pykrete", named after its champion Geoffrey Pyke), meant to cover the mid-Atlantic against U-boats | combinedops.com "Pykrete" — https://combinedops.com/Pykrete.htm ; everything-everywhere.com "Project Habakkuk" — https://everything-everywhere.com/project-habakkuk/ (search summaries: "a plan by the British during the Second World War to construct an aircraft carrier out of pykrete … a mix of ice and wood pulp … intended for use against U-boats in the mid-Atlantic") |
| 1943: a model was built on Patricia Lake, Jasper National Park, Canada | Alberta's Historic Places — https://albertashistoricplaces.com/2020/10/21/a-giant-aircraft-carrier-made-of-ice-yes-a-giant-aircraft-carrier-made-of-ice/ ; Diver Magazine "Code name: Habbakuk" — https://divermag.com/habbakuk/ (search summaries: "In January 1943, Patricia Lake was chosen as the test site for building a prototype vessel. The National Research Council coordinated the top secret construction of a model ice and sawdust ship") |
| Its wreck is still on the lake bed | same sources + kootenaymountainculture.com — https://kootenaymountainculture.com/habbakuk-sunken-ship-jasper-alberta/ ("The wooden superstructure of the ship sank to the bottom of Patricia Lake"; "a commemorative plaque along the shoreline") |
