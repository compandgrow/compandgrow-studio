# HULL 7 — facts and fiction

HULL 7 is a fictional program. The ship, its hull number, the coil spec, the power figures, the trial ranges, the side effect and the clearance are invented for the film. No real ship, agency, document or person is named or quoted.

Real background the narration leans on (general knowledge, not on-screen data):
- Degaussing: from about 1939–1940, navies (first the Royal Navy) fitted ships with coils of electrical cable to reduce their magnetic signature against magnetic mines. The line "By 1940, hulls were wrapped in cable, to cancel their magnetic field" summarises this.
- Mirages: an inferior mirage over a hot road comes from layers of air with different refractive indices bending light from the sky toward the observer.

The step from either of these to optical invisibility of a ship is not real physics.
