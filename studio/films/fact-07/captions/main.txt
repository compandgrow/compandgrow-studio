This plane flew. Its engine has no moving parts.

In 2018 MIT engineers flew a plane with a 5 m wingspan on ion wind: batteries push 40,000 volts into thin wires, the charged air becomes a wind, and the wind pushes the plane. Nearly silent, 10 flights across a gym (Nature, 21 Nov 2018).
The idea came partly from Star Trek.
Don't try this: it runs at tens of thousands of volts.
