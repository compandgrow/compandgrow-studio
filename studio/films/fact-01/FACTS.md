# FACT 01 — "Scientists made ball lightning in a lab" — facts

All claims are real; rows are reused from studio/films/ember/FACTS.md (pages opened when EMBER was made).

| Claim on screen | Source |
|---|---|
| Ball lightning: a glowing ball seen in storms | Physics (APS) 7, 5 (2014) — https://physics.aps.org/articles/v7/5 |
| 1638, Dartmoor, England (witnesses describe "a great ball of fire") | "The Great Thunderstorm" — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Great_Thunderstorm |
| 2007, Brazil: electric arc touched to a silicon wafer; glowing balls up to 4 cm, up to 8 s | Physics Today "Ball lightning in the lab" — https://physicstoday.aip.org/news/ball-lightning-in-the-lab (Paiva et al., PRL 2007) |
| 2012, western China: caught on a spectrometer; light showed silicon, iron, calcium (soil) | APS Physics 7, 5 — https://physics.aps.org/articles/v7/5 ; Physics World — https://physicsworld.com/a/burning-soil-fuels-ball-lightning/ |
