# TREMOR — facts and fiction

TREMOR is a fictional program. The 8 laser-tuned drivers, the 10/30/60 m trials, the cracked glass "for 3 blocks" and the clearance are invented for the film.

| Claim in the film | Source |
|---|---|
| 1935, Tesla (79) claims a small oscillator clamped to a girder could bring down the Empire State Building with 5 lb of air pressure | Wikipedia "Tesla's oscillator" (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tesla%27s_oscillator): at his 1935 birthday press meeting, "Five pounds of air pressure" could "destroy the Empire State Building" |
| The Houston Street lab shaking, police, the sledgehammer — "as he told it" | same article: lab at 46 East Houston Street; he reportedly ended the experiment with a sledgehammer as police arrived (Tesla's own account) |
| 2006 TV test rebuilt it; verdict busted | same article: MythBusters "Earthquake Machine" (2006), vibrations felt but no shaking of the bridge; "busted" |
| 1831, soldiers marching in step set a bridge bouncing; a bolt snapped | Wikipedia "Broughton Suspension Bridge" (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Broughton_Suspension_Bridge): 12 April 1831, 74 soldiers marching four abreast; a stay-chain bolt snapped; troops later ordered to break step on bridges |
| 1940, wind fed the Tacoma Narrows Bridge until its deck tore apart | Wikipedia "Tacoma Narrows Bridge (1940)" (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tacoma_Narrows_Bridge_(1940)): collapsed 7 Nov 1940 in ~40 mph wind from aeroelastic flutter (not simple resonance — the script says "wind feeds ... until its deck tears apart") |
