# STILLWING — treatment (style test: Exploded Teardown, film #6, Sun Nov 15)

One object the whole way: a lifter cell (foil triangle, thin wire on top, air gap) on a dark graphite seamless. It flies, comes apart into its three parts, the missing fourth part turns out to be the air, then the same cell becomes STILLWING: the cells wave-lift into a tile, the tiles into a wing, and it closes again with its dimensions drawn on it. Labels follow the style's fixed structure (accent two-digit index · name · one spec line), on leader lines from a dot on the part; no tables beside the object. Accent colour (dimensions, indices, the emitter-wire glow): one warm orange. 241 spoken words, 39 lines. Estimate 105 s at 2.3 words/s; about 115–120 s with pauses (LODESTONE: 239 words ran 118 s). Times below are estimates. Re-time them from the TTS.

**1. hook (0–10 s · a1–a5).** Frame 0 is the cover: the closed foil triangle hovering a hand's width above the seamless, soft contact shadow below, a faint violet corona on the top wire. Big text from frame 0: THIS FOIL TRIANGLE FLIES. / NOTHING ON IT MOVES. Slow orbit with nothing on the craft turning. "No wings. No rotor. No fuel." Three labels draw in and are struck out (WINGS 0 · ROTOR 0 · FUEL 0). At ~7 s "So is it antigravity?" lands as a stamp: ANTIGRAVITY? This is the question, on screen before 10 s (rule 1). "Take it apart." The first near-silence follows (room tone only, ~0.5 s).

**2. this_was_real (10–20 s · b1–b3). ONE dated beat.** The camera rises past the triangle. A thin paper sheet floats behind it in the same space, lit like a part. Label on the sheet: `01 · 1928 · British patent 300,311` / `"producing force or motion"`. "Its holder: Thomas Townsend Brown." This is text only, with no portrait and no photo. Neutral tone: he is the patent holder, nothing more. No other date in this slot. Biefeld and the later US patents stay out.

**3. physics (20–58 s · c1–c11).** The first separation, "Three parts.": the layers lift on one vertical axis, top first, 0.15 s apart (style §5). Labels: `01 EMITTER · thin wire`, `02 AIR GAP`, `03 COLLECTOR · foil skirt`. "About 30,000 volts go across them." A dimension line runs from wire to foil with `≈ 30 kV` on its plate. Dive into the gap (style "Inside the gap"): the wire glows, and small blue ion dots stream down to the foil and knock into grey air molecules. Each hit passes on a push, and the grey dots start to drift down as a wind. The wind arrows go down and the lifter arrow goes up. "The missing fourth part is the air.": the air itself gets a label, `04 AIR · the part you can't see`. The ANTIGRAVITY? stamp from the hook returns, is struck out, and ION WIND lands. Proof beat (c10–c11), *after* the physics: a fast pull-back to a drawn silhouette of a fixed-wing plane (not a photo, no marks), with labels `5 m wingspan` · `40 kV` and a dimension line `≈ 60 m · a gym's length`, plus a small source line `Source: Nature, 21 Nov 2018`. **No MIT name, logo or report cover on screen.** The ion-plane silhouette leaves frame before any STILLWING text appears. Second near-silence.

**4. program (59–73 s · d1–d4). Fiction.** Title card in screen space: STILLWING · FILE 06 (invented). "Stillwing asks: what if you stacked them?" The single cell multiplies in a wave lift (style "The wave lift"): cells into a tile, tiles into a wing. Spec labels on the object: `01 EMITTER · 0.1 mm wire` · `02 AIR GAP · 30 mm` · `03 SKIRT · foil` · `05 CELL · 30 kV` · `06 MOVING PARTS · 0`. All of these numbers are invented, and no agency, lab, university or person is named.

**5. trials (74–97 s · e1–e6). Fiction, escalating.** Each trial opens on its number (cut rule 3). TRIAL 01: one 20 cm cell lifts its own 3 g and holds 60 s on a tether, stamped FLEW. TRIAL 02: a 64-cell tile carries 2 kg and holds 4 min at 2 m, stamped FLEW. TRIAL 03: a 1,200-cell wing that closes from exploded to whole as the camera does a high three-quarter drift. Dimension line `12 km`, "own batteries" label, stamped FLEW. No sound under "Silent." except room tone.

**6. catch (98–111 s · f1–f5). Fiction that agrees with the physics.** "One catch." "It pushes on air." The wing climbs. An altitude readout runs up the right edge while the air dots around it thin out and the wind arrows shrink. `STALL · 3 km`. Cut-in through the object to a bell-jar chamber around one cell, gauge `THRUST 0`. The 0 is STILLWING's own fictional test and is never presented as a real measurement. Real tests are not mentioned in this slot.

**7. verdict (112–118 s · g1–g3).** Third near-silence, then the stamp thud: **CLEARED: BELOW 3 KM. NOT ANTIGRAVITY.** It is the same twist shape as LODESTONE's "cleared as an electric motor".

**8. outro (118–124 s · h1–h2).** Fast pull-back. The wing closes into the single foil triangle of frame 0, with its dimensions drawn on it (style ending "closed with its dimensions drawn on it"). The right third stays unseen until this pull-back. Labels: `WIRE` · `FOIL` · `AIR`. "Just wire, foil, and air." End text: CASE CLOSED / NEXT FILE OPENS SUNDAY / @compandgrow.
