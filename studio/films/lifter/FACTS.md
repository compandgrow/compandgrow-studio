# STILLWING — facts and fiction

STILLWING is a fictional program. The tile-and-wing design (cells, tiles, the 1,200-cell wing), the spec sheet (0.1 mm emitter wire, 30 mm air gap, 30 kV per cell, foil skirt), the three trials (3 g / 60 s; 2 kg / 4 min at 2 m; 12 km), the 3 km stall, the vacuum-chamber "0" reading and the stamp "CLEARED: BELOW 3 KM. NOT ANTIGRAVITY." are invented for the film and are not attributed to any real agency, lab or person. Never show STILLWING next to the names, seals or report covers of NASA, the US Army Research Laboratory, MIT, the USAF or Thomas Townsend Brown. The fiction is built to agree with the physics: the craft pushes on air, so it loses thrust as the air thins.

## Use with care
- **ARL "Force on an Asymmetric Capacitor"** (real study, real agency): it found the force, but its abstract says "ionic wind is at least three orders of magnitude too small" (simple ballistic wind) while "The ionic drift transport assumption leads to the correct order of magnitude for the force", and it calls the cause unexplained. That nuance clashes with a simple "it's just wind" line, so do **not** cite ARL for the explanation. It is fine as the source for "~30 kV" and "about 5 g" only, and need not be named on screen.
- Brown appears only as the 1928 patent holder. Say "the push turned out to be wind", never "he was wrong" or "a fraud".
- Later US patents (background only, not on screen; one date rule): US 1,974,483 "Electrostatic motor" (1934-09-25) and US 2,949,550 "Electrokinetic apparatus" (1960-08-16), per the Wikipedia patent list (links to Google Patents; Google Patents itself not opened).

## Dropped
- **Biefeld–Brown collaboration / "named after his professor"**: Wikipedia itself says "records of him even having an association with Biefeld are sketchy at best" and "Denison University claims they have no record of any such experiments" (citing Schatzkin 2023). Not used. Don't name Biefeld.
- **Application date 15 April 1927**: Wikipedia mentions a 15 Apr 1927 application for "Method of Producing Force or Motion" but does not tie it to GB 300311, and the patent record could not be opened. Use only "1928".
- **Talley "no thrust at 10^-6 torr" (1990 report)**: the 1990 report (PL/EDWARDS-TR-91-3009) was not opened. The 1988 report that was opened reached only about 10^-2 torr and saw a small residual force. Dropped. No "real tests: zero thrust in vacuum" line.
- **"Early lifters lift about 1 g per watt"**: only cited to a hobbyist page. Dropped.
- **Conspiracy / electrogravitics / UFO lore**: never-list guardrail, not researched, not used.
