This foil triangle flies. No wings. No rotor. No fuel.

It isn't antigravity. It's ion wind: about 30,000 volts set the air moving. Wind goes down, the lifter goes up.
In 2018 a real plane flew on it with no moving parts: about 60 m, the length of a gym (Nature, Nov 2018).
Don't try this: real lifters run at tens of thousands of volts.
