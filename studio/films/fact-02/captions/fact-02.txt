The US Army built a city inside the ice. 🧊

1959, Greenland: Camp Century. Tunnels cut into the ice sheet, powered by a portable nuclear reactor. Officially a science station — the secret plan was to hide missiles under the ice. Then the ice started moving. In 2024, NASA radar found it again.
