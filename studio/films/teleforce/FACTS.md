# LONGSHOT — facts and fiction

LONGSHOT is a fictional program. The pulsed "heated channel" method, the 4 cm spot, the 50/200/400 km trials, the thunderclap and radio side effect, and the clearance are invented for the film.

The historical part of the narration, with sources:

| Claim in the film | Source |
|---|---|
| Announced July 11, 1934, on Tesla's 78th birthday | PBS "Tesla: Life and Legacy" — NYT headline "TESLA, AT 78, BARES NEW 'DEATH BEAM'", 11 July 1934 (https://www.pbs.org/tesla/ll/ll_wendwar.html); Wikipedia "Teleforce" (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Teleforce) |
| "bring down 10,000 planes, 250 miles away" | PBS: "bring down a fleet of 10,000 enemy airplanes at a distance of 250 miles". (Wikipedia's text gives 200 miles; the NYT-cited figure and PBS give 250.) |
