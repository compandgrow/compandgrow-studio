Style: Engineering Briefing Board, 1080×1920, 24 fps, ~119 s, English narration (Kokoro bm_george), burned-in subtitles + .srt.
Played straight: real Tesla history (sourced in FACTS.md), then a fictional program, LONGSHOT, that "fixes" teleforce's open-air problem.

Structure (checklist down a tall wall, the reveal answers the opening tag):
1. Hook — "250 miles. 10,000 aircraft. One beam." Dimension line snaps across; ten aircraft sketches; tag "250 MI?".
2. 1934 — date, AGE 78 tag, a printed newspaper card with the real headline.
3. 1937 — six government tags clack on; red "no working beam shown".
4. 1943 — papers seized card, stamp NOTHING WORKABLE.
5. The principle — a vacuum tube accelerates a beam to near light speed (real); in open air it scatters (real).
6. The leap — LONGSHOT tower; first pulse heats a channel, later pulses ride it; beam stays tight; 4 cm at the target.
7. Trials — 50, 200, 400 km, each stamped DOWN.
8. Silence → side effect — thunderclap, radios dead within 10 km.
9. Verdict — CLEARED for field trials; "Tesla promised 250 miles. That's 400 km."; "250 MI?" struck, "400 KM" lands; pull back to the whole wall.
