# FACT #8 — the roundest objects ever made — facts

Fact short: 100% real, no fiction. Every card line must be a row below.

## Safe on screen (numbers)
- **4** quartz balls, **3.8 cm** (1.5 in) across, "ping-pong-ball size"
- **within 40 atoms** of a perfect sphere (strongest hook number)
- **1.8 × 10⁻⁷** of their diameter (average departure from a perfect sphere)
- **2004** (launched 20 Apr 2004), results **2011**
- Earth-size comparison: only as "mountains **just a couple of metres** high" — NOT 1.5 m (see Dropped)

## Dropped / changed
- **"Mountains 1.5 m high" (hook)** — sources disagree: Guinness 1.5 m, Stanford Magazine "barely six feet" (~1.8 m), Wikipedia (citing Spaceflight Now, 2004) 2.4 m. Use no exact figure; say "mountains just a couple of metres high", or lead with "within 40 atoms of a perfect sphere" (2 sources agree). Suggested hook: "These balls are round to within 40 atoms."
- **"Rounder than anything in nature"** (alternate hook) — no source says "in nature"; sources say "man-made"/"manufactured". Use "the roundest objects humans have ever made" (Guinness/Stanford wording: "most spherical man-made objects").
- **"Under 2 ten-millionths of their width"** — fine in substance, but say "1.8 ten-millionths" or show 1.8 × 10⁻⁷; "under 2" is our rounding.
- "Earth twisting space and time" — use the Stanford wording "pulls/drags space and time with it as it rotates".
