# EMBER — facts and fiction

EMBER is a fictional program. The silicon-dust feed, the magnetic cage, power drawn from the glow, the 8 s / 90 s / 6 h trials, the radio static and the clearance are invented for the film and are not attributed to any real agency, lab or person.
