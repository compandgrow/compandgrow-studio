A ball of fire floats in midair. Then it's gone. ⚡

For centuries it was only stories. In 2012, scientists recording lightning in western China finally caught one on a spectrometer: 900 m away, about 1 second. Its light showed silicon, iron and calcium, matching soil likely vaporised by the lightning strike.
